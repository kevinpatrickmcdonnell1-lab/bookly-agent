# The Bookseller's Guarantee

## Staff picks {#staff-picks}
Every staff pick carries the Bookseller's Guarantee. If you didn't love a staff pick, you can return it for store credit within 60 days of delivery, even if you read it. The credit equals the price you paid. Our booksellers would also love to suggest another book for you.

## Why store credit {#why}
The guarantee is our promise that a staff pick is worth the risk. Store credit lets you try another of our booksellers' recommendations.
