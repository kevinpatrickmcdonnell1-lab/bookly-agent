# Your account

## Password reset {#password}
If you can't log in, we can send a password reset link to the email address on your account. For your security we can only send it to the email we already have on file. To change the email on your account, a bookseller needs to verify your identity first.

## Privacy {#privacy}
We never ask for your full payment details in chat. We use your order number and email to confirm who you are.
