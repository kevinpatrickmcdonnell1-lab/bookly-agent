# Ebooks

## Downloaded ebooks {#downloaded}
Ebooks can't be returned or refunded once they have been downloaded, because the file can't be taken back. If a downloaded ebook is faulty or is the wrong title, we refund it in full.

## Ebooks not yet downloaded {#not-downloaded}
An ebook that hasn't been downloaded can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.

## Reading devices {#devices}
Bookly ebooks are EPUB files without DRM and work on most e-readers, tablets and phones. Kindle devices need the file sent through Send to Kindle.
