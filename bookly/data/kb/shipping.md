# Shipping

## Shipping in the US {#domestic}
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $35. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days for $9.99.

## International shipping {#international}
We ship to the United States and Canada only. Shipping to Canada takes 5 to 10 business days and costs $14.99 per order. Customs duties may apply. We don't ship to other countries yet.

## Tracking {#tracking}
Every physical order gets a tracking number by email when it ships. You can also ask our support team for an update at any time.

## Lost or damaged in transit {#lost}
If tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, or the carrier marks a parcel as lost, our order team investigates and sends a replacement or a refund.
